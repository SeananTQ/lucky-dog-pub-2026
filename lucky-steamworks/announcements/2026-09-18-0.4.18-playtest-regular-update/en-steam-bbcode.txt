0.4.18 Playtest Update: Cuter Dogs, Cooler Looks!

[p]Thank you for playing and sharing your feedback! The 0.4.18 Playtest update for [b][i]Lucky Dog Rise[/i][/b] brings fresh details to our dogs, along with improvements to poker interactions and the interface.[/p]
[h2]Even Cuter Dogs[/h2]
[list]
[*][p]Refined the artwork for ears, paws, and other details to give our dogs a more polished look.[/p][/*]
[*][p]Fixed eyes being positioned too far to the left, along with the resulting misalignment of glasses and hats.[/p][/*]
[*][p]Updated the chubby Shiba's face—now even rounder and cuter![/p][/*]
[*][p]Added several new dog looks, giving you more styles to collect.[/p][/*]
[*][p]Updated dog icons and some outfit item icons, making different looks easier to recognize in your inventory.[/p][/*]
[/list]
[h2]Play Poker at Your Own Pace[/h2]
[list]
[*][p]Adjusted the flow between hands to help new players understand betting, playing a hand, and collecting rewards.[/p][/*]
[*][p]Added the “Quick Start After a Win” setting. Once you're familiar with the game, turn it on to move into the next hand faster and play at your own pace.[/p][/*]
[*][p]Improved the table visuals and sound effects after collecting rewards, making the end of each hand clearer.[/p][/*]
[*][p]Adjusted tutorial prompts to make the steps easier for first-time players to follow.[/p][/*]
[/list]
[h2]Interface Improvements[/h2]
[list]
[*][p]Refined the outfit preset layout and button designs to make saving, switching, and managing looks easier.[/p][/*]
[*][p]The size adjustment prompt now makes it clear that you need to switch to desktop pet mode before changing your dog's display size.[/p][/*]
[/list]
[h2]The First Playtest Phase Is Coming to a Close[/h2]
[p]The first public Playtest phase will end on September 25, and the Playtest will then be paused. Players who already have Playtest access will keep their access entitlement; it will not be revoked. Next, we'll focus on preparing [b][i]Lucky Dog Rise Demo[/i][/b] for launch and supporting it afterward.[/p]
[p]The timing of the next Playtest phase will depend on our development and support schedule after the Demo launches. We'll share the reopening date in a future announcement once it's decided.[/p]
[p]Thank you for helping Lucky Dog grow. Your feedback helps us find issues and make the game better, one step at a time. We hope you'll try out the new looks, outfits, and poker interactions during the remaining days of this phase.[/p]
